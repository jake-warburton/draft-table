        About Draft Table

        A full three-round booster draft, dealt from the reviewed Omens set snapshot with its real collation weights — solo against bots, or live with up to eight drafters and a spectator rail. Card images are served by Legend Story Studios from their own public host; a card that cannot load its art still shows its name.
